About Us

Designing functional & modern
interior spaces

Under Space Studio is an interior design and architectural execution company based in Gurugram, delivering functional, modern and cost-efficient residential & commercial interiors across Gurgaon, Delhi, and Noida. We focus on practical layouts, clean detailing, and material transparency — not fake promises.

Our team handles concept design, 3D visualization, on-site execution, furniture, and complete project management. Clients choose us because we stay realistic with budgets, timelines, and deliverables — and we’re not afraid to say “no” when something won’t work.

Our Mission

Create intelligently-planned interiors that balance aesthetics, functionality and long-term durability — without unnecessary overselling.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted interior execution teams in NCR through transparent workflow, honest pricing, and consistent quality.

Core Values

Clear communication, accountability, disciplined project management, and commitment to delivering exactly what is promised — nothing less, nothing fake.
